Key takeaways for stopping workplace cyber threats:

  • Check sender domains and attachments: never download `.exe` files sent via spear phishing, and watch for impersonation of the MD requesting urgent wire transfers (BEC)
  • Secure your desk: follow clean-screen practice, lock your computer every time you step away, and never write passwords on notes around your desk
  • Prevent tailgating: never let anyone slip through a keycard-locked door behind you — everyone must badge in with their own credentials
  • Watch out for false positives: routine IT system notices, internal HR announcements, or ordinary reminder notes aren't always threats — panicking over everything that looks unusual is a problem too
