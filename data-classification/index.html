🔑 3 Key Takeaways from This Unit:

  1. Classify by content, not filename: A document with an ordinary-sounding title can still carry a sensitive termination-review attachment — the most sensitive content determines the classification level, not the title.
  2. Personal data is always highly sensitive: A gift-purchase record containing a personal bank account number and personal contact details counts as personal data under Thailand's PDPA, and must be classified 'Restricted'.
  3. Uphold security properties: Understanding classification levels helps us choose the right passwords and access-sharing practices, so we can each serve as the organization's first line of defense.
